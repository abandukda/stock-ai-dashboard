# ATLAS Position Management V1.1 — shadow preregistration

V1.1 replaces the never-activated V1 contract. It adds mandatory valuation-confidence
gating, preserves certified hard-thesis-break precedence over valuation persistence,
promotes governed pending-event review to the certainty gate, and introduces a closed,
versioned reason-code vocabulary. No V1 observations are migrated or synthesized.

This methodology is internal, prospective, descriptive research. It does not
change discovery Actions, certified values, publication eligibility, the Signal
Report Card, or customer UI. No personal cost basis, gain/loss, tax information,
or free-form AI judgment is an input.

The rule order is lexicographic: data certainty; hard thesis break; valuation;
technical modifier; then the current discovery Action only for derived ADD
eligibility. There is no weighted position score. Technical deterioration alone
cannot cause a long-term EXIT. News may request review but cannot set a state.

Valuation uses the already-certified lower/base/upper Fair Value uncertainty
band. A missing or incoherent band is unavailable. STRETCHED and ABOVE_FV must
persist for two certified scans before a protected instruction is emitted.
Any valuation-methodology or source-basis transition suspends protected changes
and requires governed recertification.

BUY NOW episodes enter pending-end when discovery Action leaves BUY_NOW and end
after five consecutive certified regular-session scans outside BUY_NOW. A hard
thesis EXIT ends immediately. Re-entry requires a confirmed prior end plus five
regular sessions outside BUY_NOW and fresh qualification under the then-current
discovery methodology.

The original BUY NOW record and its 1/5/21/63/126/252-session observations remain
immutable and continue regardless of later shadow states.

## Counsel checklist (questions, not legal conclusions)

- Publisher versus adviser implications and holder-directed language
- Descriptive versus imperative TRIM/EXIT terminology
- Personalized versus impersonal context
- Conflicts and employee holdings
- Hypothetical-performance treatment and recordkeeping
- Publication timing, disclosures, and data licensing

Customer activation is prohibited until the preregistered prospective-history,
event-count, scan-date, market-regime, factor/sector, compliance, and methodology
stability gates in `config/position_management_v1.json` are met.
