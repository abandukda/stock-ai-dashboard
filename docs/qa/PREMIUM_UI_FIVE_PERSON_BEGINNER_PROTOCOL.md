# Premium UI five-person beginner test protocol

## Purpose and guardrails

Test whether a first-time investor can understand the certified customer experience without coaching. Use the exact candidate SHA and governed QA artifact recorded on the test sheet. Do not change ratings, provide investment advice, expose the test data in advance, or treat a participant's preference as a methodology defect.

Each participant gets a fresh browser session, a desktop viewport (1440 px), and one mobile viewport (390 px for participants 1–3; 360 px for participants 4–5). Record task completion, wrong turns, time, confidence (1–5), and the participant's exact words. Screen recording requires consent.

## Participants and tasks

1. **Decision reader — Home to Research.** Find one BUY NOW security, open Research, and explain Action, last certified close, Fair Value, Opportunity, Confidence, and the as-of date in their own words.
2. **Financial reader — annual statements.** On one of the eleven supported securities, find revenue, one cash-flow item, and one balance-sheet item for two different fiscal years. Explain what “Unavailable” means and identify the filing accession or source hash.
3. **Risk reader — thesis to invalidation.** State what supports the case, two company-specific risks, and what would invalidate it. Then find the Evidence & Methodology disclosure.
4. **Mobile planner — Watchlist to Ask ATLAS.** At 360 px, add or inspect a Watchlist name, move through all four navigation items, open Ask ATLAS, and distinguish supporting evidence from missing evidence and limitations.
5. **Chart reader — technical/context boundaries.** Explain the stock-versus-SPY chart, a moving average, RSI, and why historical/contextual information does not change the certified decision.

## Moderator script

Say: “Please think aloud. I am testing the product, not you. Use only what is visible. I cannot explain a label until the task is over.” If a participant is stuck for 60 seconds, ask: “What would you expect to click next?” End a task at three minutes and record it as incomplete.

## Acceptance sheet

For every participant record: candidate SHA, route/ticker, viewport, task time, completion (yes/no), critical misunderstanding (yes/no), wrong turns, confidence 1–5, and verbatim notes. A critical misunderstanding is any belief that historical data, analyst context, or a chart independently changes the certified ATLAS rating.

Beginner acceptance requires all five sessions, at least four of five primary tasks completed without moderator instruction, zero critical authority misunderstandings, all participants able to locate the as-of date and limitations, and both mobile widths completing navigation without clipping. Product defects must be reproducible against the exact SHA before any patch is proposed.
