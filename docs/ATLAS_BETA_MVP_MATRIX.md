valuation, six pillars, Opportunity, Decision Confidence, Action, and

## Paid-Core activation hooks

No architecture change is required for credential installation, license-class
transition, entitlement smoke, broad certification, provider-authority review,
fresh immutable candidate generation, release certification, or prospective
Report Card activation. Paid breadth evidence, separate authority approval, and
a fresh exact candidate remain mandatory gates.

## Report Card integrity

The ledger is append-only, keeps losers and delisted names, uses SPY as the
fixed benchmark, rejects same-time/pre-decision price observations, preserves
original Action/Fair Value/reference price/methodology, separates methodology
cohorts, and represents missing future data explicitly. It is classified:

> INTERNAL METHODOLOGY VALIDATION — NOT CUSTOMER-FACING PERFORMANCE ADVERTISING
