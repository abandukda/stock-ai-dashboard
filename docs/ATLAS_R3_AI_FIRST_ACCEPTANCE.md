# ATLAS R3 AI-first acceptance contract

This is a benchmark rubric and evidence plan, not a claim of competitor parity or human acceptance. Automated conformance cannot produce a premium-design verdict. Human outcomes remain `NOT_RUN` until five real sessions and their evidence are supplied.

## Automated evidence gate

Test the exact immutable candidate at 1440×1000, 390×844, and 360×800. Preserve original screenshots for the AI-first opening, Top Ideas, and Secondary Intelligence. Every artifact must bind to the exact source SHA, have unique path and SHA-256, show no exception or horizontal overflow, and retain its original dimensions.

For each Home or Research narrative, preserve a claim manifest. Every customer claim must bind to both authoritative field IDs and evidence IDs; numeric claims without a field binding fail. Unknown identities, synthetic values, missing units, unavailable-as-zero output, provider jargon, and internal governance language fail. Automated checks assess only:

- source grounding and identity;
- ticker-to-ticker distinctness (including near-copy detection);
- concise sentence and total-length bounds;
- presence of a bottom line, one to three reasons, risks, Strengthen/Weaken/Invalidate conditions, what to watch, and freshness;
- screenshot completeness, distinctness, readability, responsive containment, and exact-SHA provenance.

## Benchmark rubric

Score each item 1–5 and attach screenshot/task evidence. Leave scores blank until review.

1. First-screen hierarchy and immediate investor takeaway.
2. Beginner comprehension of Action, Fair Value, Opportunity, and Confidence.
3. Evidence-grounded usefulness of the AI narrative.
4. Ticker-specific distinctness; no interchangeable prose.
5. Readability and scanability without loss of material risk.
6. Freshness, units, dates, citations, and unavailable states.
7. Top Ideas ranking fidelity and Research navigation ownership.
8. Desktop 1440 density and progressive disclosure.
9. Mobile 390 readability, tap targets, and overflow.
10. Mobile 360 readability, wrapping, and content priority.
11. Research/Ask claim and authority consistency.
12. Accessibility: keyboard order, visible focus, headings, names, and screen-reader announcement.

This rubric compares the experience to the stated ATLAS product contract only. It makes no claim about competitor behavior or feature parity.

## Five-person real test plan

Recruit five people who are not implementation authors: P1 novice/desktop 1440; P2 novice/mobile 390; P3 novice/mobile 360; P4 intermediate investor/desktop 1440; P5 intermediate investor/mobile (390, then a 360-width spot check). Record consent and role without sensitive personal data.

Each participant must complete the same tasks without coaching:

1. State the Morning View takeaway and identify the strongest visible opportunity.
2. Explain Action, Fair Value, Opportunity, Confidence, and evidence confidence in their own words.
3. Give two evidence-backed reasons, the primary risk, and the invalidation condition for one idea.
4. Find the evidence date/freshness and identify any unavailable field.
5. Compare two tickers and explain why their narratives are materially distinct.
6. Open the exact ticker Research page from Home and return without losing context.
7. Ask a beginner question and reconcile the answer with the displayed certified facts.

Capture per task: completion, elapsed seconds, wrong turns, assistance, exact misconception, evidence reference, and free-text comment. At session end capture 1–5 ratings for distinctness, readability, and usefulness. Preserve viewport, browser, screenshot/video reference, keyboard-only result, and screen-reader result where applicable.

## Proposed fail-closed decision rule

The product owner and independent reviewer must approve thresholds before execution. A conservative proposed gate is: 5/5 sessions completed; zero critical misconception about Action/Fair Value/Opportunity/Confidence; at least 4/5 correct unaided explanations for each protected concept; median distinctness, readability, and usefulness each at least 4/5; zero P0/P1 defects; all tasks linked to evidence. Passing completeness yields `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, never automatic human acceptance.

Required package: participant protocol/roster, task-result table, comprehension coding sheet, completed rubric, defect inventory, exact-SHA screenshot manifest, original screenshots, and session evidence references. Empty templates or self-authored simulated results remain `NOT_RUN_OR_INCOMPLETE`.
